About Kerala

Kerala is a beautiful destination in southern India known for its backwaters, beaches, hill stations, wildlife, and rich cultural heritage.

It is a popular destination for nature lovers, families, and travelers looking for a relaxing vacation.

Popular Attractions

  • Alleppey Backwaters
  • Munnar Hill Station
  • Kovalam Beach
  • Periyar Wildlife Sanctuary
  • Fort Kochi

Things to Do

  1. Enjoy a houseboat ride.
  2. Explore tea plantations.
  3. Visit beautiful beaches.
  4. Experience local cuisine.
  5. Explore traditional culture.
